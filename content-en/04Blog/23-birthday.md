## Sensitization and future messages
### Try it. Don't be afraid of mistakes.

We were taught to pursue the right rate, not blindness. We grew up to remember that lesson, and we kept it true everywhere: we had to do the right thing to go out with people, the right steps to the test, and so on. We did not realize that the so-called “right” restrictions, while allowing us to get results quickly, were a lot less likely to try, and that they were almost fatal for the engineering community. We knew how to do the right thing, but we lacked the ability to solve mistakes. That is why we often ask questions that are not so much as to describe a problem in a modest way. You can learn to drive early, just like you learn to drive, but you can't drive in a driving lesson, and the real driving starts with trying to step down a breakup. So I hope I can try more next year, not to make mistakes, to correct them, but not to try things that are interesting because I'm afraid of making mistakes, but it's like having a white paper in the examination room for fear of being kicked off.
